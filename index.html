Collaborative storytelling and comics for neurodivergent learners

Our Mission

MOSAIC helps neurodivergent learners, including adults with intellectual disabilities and autistic children, write stories together and turn them into comics.

We believe creative writing should be open to everyone, and that writers should stay in charge of their own words and ideas. We see different ways of thinking, communicating, and creating as strengths.
