Our Mission

Stories in words and in pictures

Mosaic Cognition studies how people understand and create stories, in words and in pictures, and builds tools that support that learning. Our first tool, MOSAIC, helps writers organize ideas into a story, write together, and turn their own words into comics.

We design for a wide range of learners, including neurodivergent learners such as adults with intellectual disabilities and autistic children. We believe creative writing should be open to everyone, and that writers should stay in charge of their own words and ideas.

Our Work

MOSAIC

“Organize ideas, write together, and turn your own words into comics.”

MOSAIC (Multimodal Organizer for Story Analysis, Imagery, and Comprehension) is an AI-powered storytelling tool. It helps writers organize their ideas into a story, write together with classmates, and turn their own words into comics.

MOSAIC is used in a comic literacy course at Stanford University's Cardinal Academy.
