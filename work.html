Our Work

MOSAIC (Multimodal Organizer for Story Analysis, Imagery, and Comprehension) is an AI-powered storytelling tool. It helps writers organize their ideas into a story, write together with classmates, and turn their own words into comics.

MOSAIC is used in a comic literacy course at Stanford University's Cardinal Academy.
